FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from our members and visitors.

How do I enroll as a student?

Simply visit our facility or contact us through the form above. We'll walk you through our enrollment plans and help you find the perfect fit. You can also book a free trial session to try before you join.

What subjects do you teach?

We offer 25+ subjects including maths, swimming, tennis, football, volleyball, martial arts, gymnastics, and more. Check our Services page for the full list of programs and facilities.

Do you tutor younger learners?

Absolutely! Our Early Learning program is designed for learners aged 6-17. We focus on skill development, academic study skills, and most importantly, making subjects fun and rewarding.

Can I change or cancel my learning plan?

Yes, we offer flexible month-to-month plans with no long-term contracts. You can pause or cancel your enrollment anytime by giving us 30 days notice.
